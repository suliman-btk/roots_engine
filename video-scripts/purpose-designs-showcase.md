# Purpose-Built Designs Showcase

## Scene 001

Here are ten designs, each built for a purpose. Planning narrows from many possibilities to one focus. We begin with all the content we could teach, then select what learners need, then decide the evidence we will look for, and finally arrive at one clear learning outcome.

## Scene 002

Teaching is not simply teacher-led or learner-led. Some moments call for direct explanation, others for guided practice, and others for full self-direction.

## Scene 003

Good facilitation keeps structure and freedom in balance. The teacher sets a clear outcome and a firm structure, while learners bring their own questions and choices.

## Scene 004

When you check for understanding, the evidence decides the next step. If learners understand, continue. If they are partly there, clarify. If they are confused, give another chance to practise.

## Scene 005

What we see in class is only the tip. Above the surface we notice participation and answers. Beneath the surface lie thinking, prior knowledge, feelings, and confidence.

## Scene 006

ROOTS is built on five domains and fifteen principles, delivered across 25 lessons.

## Scene 007

Planning, teaching, and assessment work like gears. When planning turns, teaching moves, and assessment drives the next decision.

## Scene 008

Learner-centred teaching means focusing on learners' needs, their participation, the evidence they show, and the outcomes they reach.
