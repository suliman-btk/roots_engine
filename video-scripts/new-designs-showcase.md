# New card designs showcase

## Opening

This short preview shows the new card designs, the new animations, and the new transitions between scenes.

## Bar chart

Bars grow one by one, and each value counts up as it appears. Here, practice rises from awareness to confident daily use.

## Donut chart

A donut chart shows how one whole divides into parts. Each segment draws in turn, and the centre shows the active value.

## Pyramid

A pyramid builds from the foundation upward. Each layer rests on the one beneath it, from shared values to lasting impact.

## Venn diagram

A Venn diagram shows where ideas overlap. Knowledge, skills, and attitudes meet in the centre, where real competence lives.

## Mind map

A mind map places one central idea in the middle, and branches draw out to each connected idea around it.

## Closing

These five designs join the existing cards, so every lesson can now move with more variety and a clearer story.
